制作方法：内蔵画像生成で編集
Precise manga edit FIRST PANEL ONLY. Preserve characters, all art and panels2-4, title/footer, ALL dialogue wording and all other balloon positions.
A) Enlarge Haru's lower-left vertical speech balloon 「この問題、わかんない……」 by about 15 percent, mostly UPWARD and slightly left, making its top noticeably higher than Sumi's lower-right balloon and making it visibly more prominent. Keep close to orange Haru, no faces/notebook/hands obscured. Keep exact two-column vertical Japanese type RIGHT 「この問題、」 LEFT 「わかんない……」 with generous space. Maintain clear short tail to Haru.
B) Sumi lower-right balloon: COMPLETELY ERASE its current lettering, including stray horizontal bar overlapping ま. Then freshly render clean standard printed Japanese Gothic text 「どこまでわかった？」 TWO vertical columns: RIGHT column top-to-bottom ど / こ / ま / で, LEFT column わ / か / っ / た / ？. Each glyph in separate evenly spaced square, no glyphs touching. Make ま unmistakable standard hiragana with exactly its normal two horizontal strokes, clean vertical stem and round bottom loop. Remove ALL extra overlapping horizontal strokes; こ above and で below must have generous gap from ま. No hand-drawn distorted letters. Keep Sumi balloon size/location/tail unchanged. Nothing else changes.
